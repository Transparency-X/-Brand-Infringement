# IP Law Firms

### 🌍 Regional Requirements

Intellectual property and brand protection are fundamentally **territorial**. This means:

*   **US law firms** are indispensable for US copyright (DMCA), trademark (USPTO), and Right of Publicity claims.
*   **UK law firms** are needed for UK copyright, trademark (UKIPO), and post-Brexit EU matters.
*   **EU law firms** (with a presence in key jurisdictions like Germany, France, or the Netherlands) are essential for EU trademarks (EUIPO) and navigating the Digital Services Act (DSA).
*   **Australian law firms** are necessary for Australian trademark, copyright, and consumer law matters.

Furthermore, different law firms have distinct specializations. Some excel at high-volume DMCA takedowns, while others are leaders in AI/deepfake law or litigation against counterfeiters. Your final selection should be based on a firm's specific expertise that aligns with your platform's top infringement priorities.
