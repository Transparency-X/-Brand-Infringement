## 1. Introduction & Background

QueenGuard is a new multi-layered, AI-driven brand and persona protection platform built exclusively for drag entertainers and the agencies, bookers, and media that work with them. The platform monitors a wide range of infringements—impersonation, unauthorised use of name/likeness, deepfakes, counterfeit merchandise, domain squatting, false endorsements, and reputational damage—and provides automated detection, guided takedown workflows, and verified-badge trust signals.

We are now seeking to engage specialist IP and entertainment law firms in our key markets (US, UK, EU, Australia) to build the foundational legal architecture that will shield the platform from liability, ensure full regulatory compliance, and create robust, defensible takedown procedures.

---

## 2. Project Objectives

- Develop a legally sound, multi-jurisdictional framework that allows QueenGuard to operate as a neutral intermediary, facilitating infringement detection and takedowns without exposing itself to undue liability.
- Create all necessary user-facing and internal legal documentation (Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Takedown Playbook, etc.).
- Ensure full compliance with relevant copyright, trademark, right of publicity, data protection, and platform intermediary laws in the US (DMCA, CDA §230), UK, EU (Digital Services Act, GDPR), and Australia.
- Produce a practical “Takedown Playbook” that non-lawyer team members can follow when handling detected infringements.
- Advise on the structure of white-label and agency partnership agreements for the product’s enterprise tier.
- Provide preliminary guidance on emerging legal risks around AI-generated content (deepfakes) and optional blockchain verification features.

## 3. Scope of Services

We envisage the engagement in four phases. Please feel free to propose adjustments based on your firm’s expertise.

### Phase 1: Foundational Legal Documentation & Takedown Framework
*Estimated timeline: 2–3 months from kick-off*

**A. Terms of Service / User Agreement**
- Draft a platform-wide Terms of Service covering:
  - Individual drag performers (opt-in, free and paid tiers).
  - Agency users (white-label dashboard, roster management).
  - Booker/media users (subscription access, verification reliance).
- Include clear provisions on:
  - User obligations and representations (e.g., that they own the rights they claim).
  - Limitation of liability and indemnification of QueenGuard.
  - Disclaimers regarding the accuracy of “verified” badges and the platform’s role as intermediary.
  - Jurisdiction-specific clauses for US, UK, EU, and Australian users.

**B. Privacy Policy & Data Processing**
- Draft a comprehensive Privacy Policy compliant with GDPR (UK & EU variants), CCPA/CPRA (US), and Australian Privacy Act.
- Address data collection from AI monitoring, social media scraping, user-uploaded evidence, and third-party integrations.
- Include cookie policy, data subject rights, and cross-border data transfer mechanisms.

**C. DMCA & Intermediary Liability Compliance**
- Register a designated DMCA agent with the US Copyright Office (if we act as service provider).
- Draft a repeat infringer policy and internal DMCA handling procedures.
- Advise on equivalent notice-and-takedown compliance in the UK/EU/Australia (e.g., DSA trusted flagger status, eCommerce Directive, UK Online Safety Act implications).

**D. Takedown Playbook**
- Create a step-by-step operational manual for the QueenGuard team covering:
  - How to verify a detected infringement before acting.
  - Which laws apply to each infringement type (copyright, trademark, right of publicity, defamation).
  - How to file DMCA/subpoena-equivalent requests on major platforms (Instagram, TikTok, Etsy, Redbubble, etc.).
  - Templates for cease-and-desist letters, DMCA takedown notices, UDRP complaints, and platform report forms.
  - Escalation procedures for non-responsive platforms or repeat infringers.
  - Guidance on preserving evidence for potential litigation.

**E. Platform Liability & Indemnification Framework**
- Analyse QueenGuard’s exposure under laws such as CDA §230, the EU Digital Services Act, and Australian defamation law.
- Draft internal guidelines for human review of AI-flagged content to maintain intermediary protections.
- Draft user indemnification clauses and, if appropriate, recommend insurance coverage for the platform.

### Phase 2: Multi-Jurisdictional Compliance & Right of Publicity Review
*Estimated timeline: 1–2 months (can run partly in parallel with Phase 1)*

- Provide a jurisdictional memorandum on the right of publicity / personality rights in the US (state-by-state critical variations), UK (passing off / misuse of private information), key EU member states, and Australia.
- Advise on how QueenGuard’s “verified badge” system and database directory interact with these rights.
- Address cross-border enforcement challenges and recommended local counsel networks for litigation.

### Phase 3: Agency White-Label & Partnership Agreements
*Estimated timeline: 1 month*

- Draft a master services agreement for agencies that wish to white-label QueenGuard as their own “Agency Protect” service.
- Include terms for:
  - Revenue sharing / commission on subscription fees.
  - Data handling and ownership of client lists.
  - Co-branding guidelines and liability allocation.
- Draft a template Data Processing Addendum (DPA) for agency partners handling EU/UK performer data.

### Phase 4: Emerging Issues Advisory (Optional, Scoped Separately)
- Provide a short memorandum on:
  - Legal risks and mitigation strategies for AI detection of deepfakes (false positives, potential defamation claims).
  - Implications of the EU AI Act for automated content flagging.
  - Blockchain-based verification badges (NFTs, smart contracts) and intellectual property / consumer protection considerations.
